Background

on site — inverter & battery commissioning

I hold a Master's degree in Microgrid Control enhanced with AI methods, and I've worked across the full spectrum of solar — from A to Z — with clients across multiple countries throughout the Middle East. That end-to-end exposure gives me a complete frame for solar projects: I've been hands-on through every single step of the design and delivery cycle, from feasibility and PV sizing through PVsyst/HelioScope/HOMER simulation, electrical SLDs, BoQs, and site execution.

My most recent role has been as a Senior After-Sales Engineer, resolving critical technical escalations on residential and commercial solar systems. Before that, I worked as an Electrical Design Engineer, developing low-voltage electrical designs and single-line diagrams. All projects shown here were delivered by me as the design engineer for various solar/EPC companies across the region — client and company identities are withheld for confidentiality.

02 — expertise

What I do

PV system design & sizing

Array configuration, string sizing, tilt and orientation, and capacity planning against real load and irradiance data.

PVsyst & HelioScope simulation

Yield modelling, loss diagrams, performance ratio analysis, and P50/P90 production probability.

Single-line & electrical diagrams

Full electrical distribution drawings for PV, hybrid, and battery-backed systems, ready for site execution.

Hybrid PV-diesel & battery systems

Fuel-saving controller integration, multi-inverter interfacing (Modbus/Ethernet), and generator load coordination.

Structural mounting design

Galvanized steel racking layouts sized to site geometry, wind, and snow load requirements.

BoQ & cost estimation

Bills of quantities, component specification, and cabling schedules for accurate bid preparation.
